## WHAT I FOUND THAT THE PUBLIC AND THE BOTH SIDES MISSED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ordered by how much I think it matters.

**1. THE UNEXPLAINED PERCENTAGE IS AN INTAKE ARTIFACT, AND THE PROOF IS A COMMITTEE DATE.**
The rate fell from >20% to a few percent *immediately after the 1953 Robertson Panel* - i.e.
when they stopped asking. The mechanism is Battelle's decision rule: TWO analysts must agree to
call a case identified, ALL FOUR must agree to call it unidentified. Any disagreement converts a
known object into an unknown one. And in the worst-quality reports the IDENTIFIED rate *rises* to
an identical 70% for military and civilian - the less information a case holds, the more likely
it is called identified, which is gap-filling, not sky-watching. Nobody has ever inverted that
table. Decisive addition: Valle's own federally funded 260,000-case corpus carries a **95%
discard threshold**, and the ~13,000 cases that survive have never been analysed. AARO's
unresolved corpus is 205 cases out of an uncurated intake, at 64% - or 40% in the same year in
the same tranches. Same phenomenon, different intake.

**2. UAP GENERATION IS A DOCUMENTED, MULTI-DECADE, MULTI-ACTOR PRACTICE. NOBODY TESTS CASES

## WHERE I WAS WRONG, AND I AM RECORDING IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- **I overstated the Loeb governance line.** "Reports through a governance board he has no
  access to" was one inferential step too far. The record supports something narrower and worse:
  the council has a board above it, and when its most senior member was asked whom it reports to,
  **he did not say.** The non-answer is the flag.
- **I falsified my own H3.** The nuclear-cluster hypothesis does not survive its own test. Run
  against an index I did not build, unresolved multi-witness cases sit at EM/nuclear facilities
  about 25-30% of the time - roughly what you would predict from **where the instrumented
  witnesses are**. It is a selection effect in the reporting apparatus. The narrow survivor
  (high-power EM/nuclear test activity generating unclassifiable artifacts) is real and is twice
  attested in the USAF's own test record - an EMP simulator down silo cables, a decoy filmed
  from a mountain - and the LIPF patent is a documented, active, unchecked capability.
- **I mis-stated the Haynesville energy figure.** I logged Valle's "500 MW to 1,400 MW" as if
  established. **The paper says 500-900 MW**, and its own abstract says the input is
  "witness-described apparent angular diameters and brightness estimates" at "a range of
  plausible distances." Galloway's own estimate was ~1 MW. The headline number moved three
  orders of magnitude on the assumed distance, not on any new observation. **Nobody quotes the
  method, including most write-ups of the paper.** The claim is smaller and more interesting than
  its reputation: given a witness's brightness and distance impressions, the implied power is
  inconsistent with 1960s conventional and natural sources.
- **I used two sources whose reliability I then documented against.** The Vallée interview
  transcript is machine-generated (FreeTranscribe.org, speaker labels omitted) and hosted on
  ufopedia.fyi, an advocacy-adjacent archive. InsiderRelease is **AI-generated on every page, by
  its own disclaimer** - I used it as an index to primaries (AARO's own text, Congressional
  transcripts, Elsevier, state registries), not as an authority. One of the nine hypotheses I
  started with turned out to rest on a source that is itself an instance of the thing I was
  studying.

## WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND, SPECIFICALLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not "better evidence." These five, in order of cost:
1. **Comparative gamma spectrometry on the two 1966 Haynesville bark samples** - blackened side
   vs normal side of the same trunk. Two days, five figures. A genuine differential
   radionuclide enrichment would be the hardest anomaly in the record; equal activity collapses
   the whole thing into 1960s fallout and a surface burn.
2. **Trajectory reconstruction of the Release 02 "UFO shot down" video** from the shooting
   platform. A physical interaction is the only thing that separates an object from a lens
   artifact, a bird, a balloon or a plasma decoy.
3. **The Manzano 1980 AFOSI file** - specifically the instrument record behind "a type unknown
   to their electronical equipment." That single phrase is the most interesting sentence in the
   official record and it has never been read by anyone outside a classified room.
4. **Any Grusch claim with a documentable chain of custody for one physical object.** The claim
   is structurally unfalsifiable as currently constituted because it requires an insider and
   every identified insider has declined. One object with provenance would change that.
5. **A technosignature detection under a handshake model rather than a beacon model.** The
   beacon-model null is one of the strongest results in science and it does not apply to a
   72-second non-repeating transmission. Nobody has built the instrument.
