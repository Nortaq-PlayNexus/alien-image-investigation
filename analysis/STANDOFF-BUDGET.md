# THE STANDOFF BUDGET — why the "directed energy" mechanism question is contested,
# and what it predicts. Written because the Kostinsky optoacoustic proposal (Jan 2026)
# and the 60 Minutes device description (Mar 2026) are two different device classes,
# and they have OPPOSITE standoff economics.

## 1. THE QUESTION IS ABOUT STANDOFF, NOT POWER
Everything about AHI / "targeted individual" claims turns on one geometric fact: can a
directed-energy device produce a perceivable effect at 100-300 m? The claimed range in
2024 reporting was "500 to 1,000 yards" (150-300 m); the 2026 60 Minutes device
description says "the range of the beam is several hundred feet" and it "can penetrate
windows and drywall."

## 2. CLASS A — PULSED MICROWAVE / RF. Fails on geometry alone.
The microwave auditory effect is REAL and well demonstrated. Mechanism: pulsed RF absorbed in
tissue produces thermoelastic expansion, generating an acoustic transient at the pulse
repetition rate, heard as a tone or buzz.

The standoff constraint is not about power. It is that the effect requires the head to be
within about half a wavelength of the radiating aperture:
    d < lambda/2 = c / (2 f)
    f = 1 GHz   -> lambda = 0.300 m -> d < 0.15 m
    f = 2.45 GHz -> lambda = 0.122 m -> d < 0.06 m
    f = 30 GHz   -> lambda = 0.010 m -> d < 5 mm
Reported AHI standoffs are 150-300 m. That is 1,000x to 6,000x beyond the Class A limit.
>>> JASON (2022) did not need the dose argument. The geometry alone excludes 500 MHz-30 GHz
    for any case not involving a transmitter within centimetres of the victim. I was wrong in
    window 2 to have called the frequency exclusion "strongly motivated by the inability to
    achieve the required EM field intensities" - it is far more simply a half-wavelength
    constraint, and that is a stronger and cleaner reason than the one usually given.

CONSEQUENCE: A REAL microwave auditory effect implies the transmitter is IN THE ROOM, or
immediately outside it. That is consistent with the 2018 FBI finding (malfunctioning
surveillance equipment) and with the JASON insect finding, and it is DECISIVE against a
vehicle-launched or embassy-adjacent emitter. The 60 Minutes device as described (backpack,
several hundred feet) does NOT fit Class A auditory effects at 100 m. Either the device
description is loose, or the audible effect is not microwave auditory.

## 3. CLASS B - PULSED IR LASER, OPTOACOUSTIC. Fails nowhere obvious.
Kostinsky (Jan 2026, Nuclear Physics Institute, Czech Academy of Sciences) proposes the
optoacoustic effect: pulsed optical energy absorbed near the target produces thermal expansion
and a local acoustic transient. The crucial asymmetry:

    THE ACOUSTIC WAVE IS GENERATED AT THE TARGET, NOT TRANSMITTED TO IT.

So the acoustic amplitude has no propagation loss. The only loss is optical extinction between
emitter and target. In a 1.5-2.0 um window, clear-air extinction is roughly 0.1-0.5 dB/km, so
300 m costs well under 0.2 dB. Standoff in Class B is limited by beam divergence, optics
size, and line of sight - NOT by physics of the target interaction.

>>> THAT IS WHY THE MARCH 2026 REVIEW FOUND NO SPECIFIC TECHNICAL OBJECTIONS FROM SCIENTISTS
    WHO HAD WORKED ON AHI. They were not endorsing the hypothesis. They were observing that the
    standard objection - no standoff - does not apply to this mechanism. That is a meaningful
    and under-reported distinction.

## 4. WHAT CLASS B AND CLASS A CAN AND CANNOT EXPLAIN
CAN explain: a localised, directional, persistent sound percept. Both mechanisms generate the
sound at the target, so directionality and localisation are unproblematic. The "screeching,
chirping, clicking, or piercing" quality with "a specific direction" is exactly what a
locally-generated acoustic transient looks like.

CANNOT currently explain: the reported PHYSIOLOGICAL sequelae. This is the honest limit and it
is the whole ballgame.
 - For a 90 dB SPL pulsed percept, single-mechanism acoustic trauma is a real concern at
   140 dB, and noise-induced hearing loss at 85 dB sustained. Neither produces acute cognitive
   disruption, hair loss, or neoplasia.
 - NIH (2024) found no MRI-detectable difference between 86 AHI patients and controls, and
   could not replicate the 2018 UPenn result.
 - A Norwegian government scientist self-tested a Class A device in 2024 and reported
   neurological symptoms not identical to AHI. That is one uncontrolled self-experiment and
   cannot separate the device effect from expectation. It is suggestive, not probative.
>>> NET: THE NOISE IS MECHANICALLY PLAUSIBLE AND THE INJURY IS NOT ESTABLISHED. Every serious
    version of this claim - Cuban, Russian, American - requires the same asymmetry: a real
    stimulus, an unproven physiological consequence, and a stress/nocebo contribution that
    Bartholomew and Baloh documented in 2023 and that the 2023 ODNI assessment adopted
    ("a socially constructed catch-all category for an array of pre-existing health conditions,
    responses to environmental factors, and stress reactions").

## 5. WHAT THIS PREDICTS, AND HOW IT WOULD BE TESTED
P1. The sound should be reproducible on demand, correlated in time with an operator action, and
    should stop when the emitter stops. A real emitter implies a control channel.
P2. A directional audio recording of an AHI/TI event, taken by someone who is NOT a subject,
    in a location where the emitter is not present, should contain the signature. If the sound
    is only ever present in the account and never in the ambience, that is decisive and it is
    testable by anyone with a microphone and a friend who does not hear it.
P3. The Stargate-style protocol result is the relevant prior: a multi-station audio capture with
    blind listening. It is cheap. It is the obvious experiment and it is the one that would settle
    the auditory question, and I am not aware of it having been run on any AHI or TI case.
P4. Dose-response: if a Class A or Class B emitter is real, the effect should scale with
    distance and with the emitter's output setting, measurable by anyone who can be near a
    device. That is the experiment the Norwegian scientist ran on himself, and it is the right
    experiment, and it should be run with blinded symptom reporting and a sham control.

## 6. WHY THIS MATTERS TO THE UFO CASE SPECIFICALLY
It does not. That is the point of including it. The Condign plasma mechanism, the LIPF decoy
patent, and the optoacoustic device are three DIFFERENT electromagnetic phenomena proposed for
three different corpora, and the discourse collapses all of them into "electromagnetic
phenomena = aliens." They are not the same claim. The nearest common structure is shared:
    a generated EM field, localised in space and time, with a perceivable signature and a
    contested physiological claim.
The valuable move is to KEEP THEM SEPARATE, because they have different standoff economics,
different test protocols, and different institutions holding the data. Collapsing them is how
a 1978 MoD working paper on plasma, a 2022 Navy patent on decoys, and a 2026 Czech Academy
proposal on lasers ended up looking like one big cover-up.
